## 5. International transfers
Your data is held and processed outside Israel. The Ranky application runs on Render in Frankfurt, Germany. Our database (Neon, PostgreSQL) runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-2, Ohio), so account, business, scan and billing-reference data are stored in the US. Email delivery (Resend), our CDN and bot protection (Cloudflare) and the AI providers that receive scan questions (OpenAI, Google) are US companies or operate from the US. The AI providers receive only a business category and a city, not your email, name or website. Apart from payment data held by SUMIT in Israel, your data is therefore held in Germany and the United States, not only in the EU.
These transfers are subject to the Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001. We use the processors listed above to provide the service. They process data on our behalf. We have data-processing agreements, or the provider's standard data-processing terms, in place with our service providers (Render, Neon, Resend, Cloudflare, SUMIT, OpenAI, Google and cron-job.org), and we may rely on them for these transfers.

## 6. Retention
Account and scan data: while your account is active and up to 24 months after closure, after which we delete or anonymise it. Deletion is done manually: you may ask us at any time to delete your data (subject to legal retention duties), and we handle each request within 30 days. Billing and receipt records: for the period required by Israeli tax law. Server and CDN request logs are kept by the hosting providers for a limited period set by their plans (generally days to a few weeks). You may ask us to delete your account earlier, subject to legal retention duties.

## 7. Security

## 10. Cookies and similar technologies
We use only strictly necessary and functional storage: a language-preference cookie and a currency-preference cookie (set only when you switch language or currency), browser session storage to carry your scan or dashboard session, and local storage to remember your cookie-notice choice and your accessibility settings. Cloudflare Turnstile (bot protection) and, on the card step of checkout, SUMIT's payment script may use their own storage. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use any third-party tracker. The full list, durations and how to control them are in the [Cookie Policy](/en/legal/cookies).
