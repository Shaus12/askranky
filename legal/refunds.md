## 2. Israeli consumers — cancellation of a distance transaction

- We refund the amount paid within 14 days of the cancellation notice, to the same card, **less a cancellation fee of 5% of the price or ILS 100, whichever is lower**, [and less the value of service actually provided until cancellation — CONFIRM with counsel; for continuous services the law allows charging for the period used].

- For a continuous-service transaction, cancellation can be done at any time; charges stop within 3 business days of notice (section 13D).
Business customers (purchasing not for personal use) may not have statutory consumer rights, but section 3 still applies.
