# Refund & Cancellation Policy — Ranky
Last updated: 2026-10-23. Operated by Shai Shotashvili, Exempt Dealer (Osek Patur) No. 322713082, Etzel 8, Bat Yam, Israel.

## 1. Cancelling your subscription (standing order)
- Cancel anytime: dashboard → Cancel subscription, or email shaysho1111@gmail.com (from your account email).
- We cancel the standing order with our processor (SUMIT) and confirm in writing by email, including the date charges stop (at the latest within 3 business days).
- No further charges after cancellation. You keep access until the end of the current paid month. Partial months are not refunded unless section 2 or 3 applies.

## 2. Israeli consumers: cancelling a distance transaction
Under section 14C of the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, a consumer may cancel within **14 days** from the transaction (or from receipt of the written disclosure, whichever is later), by written notice (an email to shaysho1111@gmail.com is sufficient).
- We refund the amount paid within 14 days of the cancellation notice, to the same card, **less a cancellation fee of up to 5% of the price or ILS 100, whichever is lower**.
- Additional protections under the law for certain consumers (such as people aged 65+, people with disabilities and new immigrants) apply, and we honour them.
- For a continuous service, you may cancel at any time; charges stop within 3 business days of your notice.
Business customers (purchasing not for personal use) may not have statutory consumer rights, but section 3 still applies to them.

## 3. Refunds for errors
If a scan failed, you were charged in error, or you were charged after cancelling, email shaysho1111@gmail.com within 14 days and we will refund that charge in full.

## 4. How refunds are issued
Refunds go to the original card via SUMIT, and you receive written confirmation and the accounting document for the refund (no VAT is involved: we are an exempt dealer, עוסק פטור). Your bank may take a few business days to show it; if your card is in a different currency than the charge, exchange-rate differences may apply.

## 5. Failed payments
If a renewal fails we email you and the processor may retry. If it remains unpaid after 7 days, the account moves to the free tier; your data is kept and no collection fees are charged.

## 6. Customers outside Israel
The same policy applies. You can always cancel online without contacting support.
