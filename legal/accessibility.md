# Accessibility Statement — Ranky
Last updated: 2026-10-23 (version 1.1)
The Hebrew version of this statement prevails over the English translation if they differ.

Shai Shotashvili (Exempt Dealer (Osek Patur) No. 322713082) is committed to equal access to Ranky for all customers, including people with disabilities, in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013.

## Standard and current status
We are working to make askranky.com meet Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA, and we aim for WCAG 2.1 level AA where we can. The site has not yet undergone a formal external accessibility audit; the measures below come from our own review, and some parts may not yet be fully accessible.

## What we have done
- Each page declares its language, and Hebrew pages are laid out right-to-left.
- Visible keyboard focus indicators on links, buttons and form fields.
- Animations are reduced for visitors who set "reduce motion" in their system.
- An accessibility menu (the floating button at the corner of every page) lets you enlarge or reduce text, switch to high contrast or light contrast, use grayscale, highlight links, use a more readable font, stop animations and use a larger cursor. Your choices are saved only in your browser. It is an aid on top of the site's own accessibility work, not a substitute for it.
- A cookie notice that does not block the page and can be used with a keyboard and screen reader.
- Responsive layout for phones and tablets.

## Known limitations
- The bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) is provided by a third party. If it is a barrier for you, email us and we will run the scan for you manually as an alternative.
- Visual elements in reports, such as the score gauge and charts, may not yet be fully accessible to screen readers. Email us and we will send you the same data in an accessible format.
- The card fields on the checkout page are served by our payment processor, SUMIT (embedded in our page), and their accessibility is partly outside our control. If you cannot complete a payment, email us and we will help you find another way to subscribe.

## Physical accessibility
Ranky is an online-only service with no public premises.
