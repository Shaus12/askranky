# Refund & Cancellation Policy — Ranky
Last updated: 2026-10-27 (version 1.2). Operated by Shai Shotashvili, Exempt Dealer (Osek Patur) No. 322713082, Etzel 8, Bat Yam, Israel.

## 1. Cancelling your subscription (standing order)
- Cancel anytime: dashboard → Cancel subscription, or email shaysho1111@gmail.com (from your account email).
- We cancel the standing order with our processor (SUMIT) and confirm in writing by email, including the date charges stop (at the latest within 3 business days).
- No further charges after cancellation. You keep access until the end of the current paid month. Partial months are not refunded unless section 2 or 3 applies.

## 1A. One-time Fix-up
- The Fix-up is a single payment; there is no standing order and nothing to cancel later.
- **Deliver-or-refund:** if we have not handed over your three fixes within 14 days of payment, or have not run the re-scan within 45 days of payment, you get a full refund on request.
- Before we hand over the fixes you may cancel for a full refund. Israeli consumers also keep the rights in section 2; after the fixes have been handed over, a cancellation under section 2 is refunded in proportion to the part of the service not yet provided (the re-scan), as the law allows.

## 1B. Founding members and the 30-day deliver-or-refund promise
- Founding members: the first 20 paying customers (the number shown on the pricing page at the time of purchase) keep the Starter price they signed up at (₪79/month) for as long as their subscription stays active without interruption. If the subscription ends, the then-current price applies to a new one.
- Deliver-or-refund: if within 30 days of your first monthly payment we have not delivered your full report, your three fixes and one re-scan, we refund that first payment in full on request (email us within 45 days of the payment).

## 2. Israeli consumers: cancelling a distance transaction
Under section 14C of the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, a consumer may cancel within **14 days** from the transaction (or from receipt of the written disclosure, whichever is later), by written notice (an email to shaysho1111@gmail.com is sufficient).
- We refund the amount paid within 14 days of the cancellation notice, to the same card, **less a cancellation fee of up to 5% of the transaction value, capped at ILS 100**, where the law permits a fee (see "Cancellation fee" below).
- Additional protections under the law for certain consumers (such as people aged 65+, people with disabilities and new immigrants) apply, and we honour them.
- For a continuous service, you may cancel at any time; charges stop within 3 business days of your notice.
Business customers (purchasing not for personal use) may not have statutory consumer rights, but section 3 still applies to them.

### Cancellation fee
Where the law permits, a cancellation fee of up to 5% of the transaction value, capped at ILS 100, may be deducted from a refund. The fee applies only where the law permits and does not reduce your statutory rights. In particular, no cancellation fee is charged where the service is defective or does not match what was promised, for the errors described in section 3, or in any other case where the law does not allow a fee. The fee is applied manually when we process a refund; it is not charged automatically.

## 3. Refunds for errors
If a scan failed, you were charged in error, or you were charged after cancelling, email shaysho1111@gmail.com within 14 days and we will refund that charge in full, with no cancellation fee.

## 4. How refunds are issued
Refunds go to the original card via SUMIT, and you receive written confirmation and the accounting document for the refund (no VAT is involved: we are an exempt dealer, עוסק פטור). Your bank may take a few business days to show it; if your card is in a different currency than the charge, exchange-rate differences may apply.

## 5. Failed payments
If a renewal fails we email you and the processor may retry. You then have a grace period of 7 days from the failed charge. If it remains unpaid after the 7-day grace period, the account moves to the free tier; your data is kept and no collection fees are charged.

## 6. Customers outside Israel
The same policy applies. You can always cancel online without contacting support.
